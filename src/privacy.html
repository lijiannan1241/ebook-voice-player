Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026 · applies to the {{APP_NAME}} Windows application and this website.

The short version

  • No account is required, and none is offered.
  • Your books, bookmarks and notes stay on your device.
  • Only text you choose to narrate is sent to the online speech service.
  • The app contains no telemetry, analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • This website sets no cookies and loads no third-party resources.

What the application stores on your device

{{APP_NAME}} stores the following data in your Windows user profile:

  • Settings — chosen voice, speech rate, interface language, text size and similar preferences.
  • Reading data — your position in each book, bookmarks and notes.
  • Audio cache — generated speech files, kept so that replaying a paragraph does not require downloading it again.

Books themselves are opened in place and are not copied into a separate library. Removing the app and deleting its data folder under %APPDATA% removes the data listed above.

What is sent over the network

When you press play or export audio, the text selected for narration is sent to the Microsoft Edge online text-to-speech service in order to generate speech. The request contains the text to be spoken, the selected voice name and the speaking rate.

This processing is necessary to provide the feature you requested. Microsoft's handling of data in that service is described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement. {{APP_NAME}} does not receive or store a copy of the generated audio on any server operated by the publisher.

Book files, bookmarks, notes and reading positions are not uploaded. The app makes no other outbound connections for advertising, analytics or crash reporting.

Exported audio files

MP3 files you export are written to the folder you choose. They remain under your control and are not uploaded anywhere by the app.

Website

This site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not set cookies, does not use analytics, and does not load fonts, scripts or images from third parties. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may process standard technical request data such as IP addresses in server logs; see the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Children

{{APP_NAME}} is a general-purpose reading tool and is not directed at children under 13. The app does not collect personal information from any user.

Your choices

  • You can avoid all network requests for narration by not pressing play and by not exporting audio.
  • You can delete the local audio cache and reading data at any time from the app or from your user profile.
  • You can uninstall the app through Windows Settings → Apps.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes in the Microsoft Store.
