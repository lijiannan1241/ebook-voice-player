      <h2>Three Training Modes: One Book, Three Speed Profiles</h2>

      <h3>Mode A: Extensive / Commute Ear Training (Speed 1.1-1.3×)</h3>
      <ol>
        <li>Voice: Aria / Guy / Sonia (Standard Broadcast)</li>
        <li>Speed: 1.1× Start, Push to 1.2-1.3× After Adaptation</li>
        <li>Export: Full Book Chapter-Split MP3 → Phone Smart AudioBook Player Speed Playback</li>
        <li>Goal: Build Language Sense, Accumulate Vocab in Context, Train Listening Stamina</li>
      </ol>

      <h3>Mode B: Intensive / Sentence-by-Sentence (Speed 0.6-0.8× + Sentence Loop)</h3>
      <ol>
        <li>Voice: Aria / Jenny (Clearest)</li>
        <li>Speed: 0.7× (Slow Enough to Catch Linking, Reduction, Elision)</li>
        <li>Workflow: Paragraph Highlight Follow → Unclear Sentence → "Sentence Loop" (Right-Click/Shortcut) → Dictation/Shadowing</li>
        <li>Vocab: Select Unknown → Right-Click "Lookup/Note" → Export Notes with Context → Import Anki/Notion</li>
        <li>Goal: Conquer Linking/Reduction, Grammar Structures, Vocab in Context, Pronunciation Mimicry</li>
      </ol>

      <h3>Mode C: Shadowing / Echo Reading (Speed 0.9-1.0× + Paragraph Loop)</h3>
      <ol>
        <li>Voice: Jenny / Jane (Natural, Warm, Emotive)</li>
        <li>Speed: 0.9× (Leaves Window to Speak Along)</li>
        <li>Workflow: Play Sentence → Pause → Repeat/Shadow → Compare Recording (Phone Voice Memo)</li>
        <li>Advanced: Enable "Paragraph Loop", Same Passage 3-5× Until Fluent</li>
        <li>Goal: Fluency, Prosody Feel, Muscle Memory, Speaking Output</li>
      </ol>

      <ol>
        <li>Drag into Ebook Voice Player → Right Chapter Tree Check: <code>Chapter 1</code>, <code>Chapter 2</code>... No Disorder</li>
        <li>Keep Chapter Numbers (<code>Chapter 1: Introduction</code>) for Note Mapping</li>

      <h3>3. Intensive Settings & Single-Chapter Export (10 Min)</h3>
      <ol>
        <li>Select Target Chapter → Right-Click "Export Audio" → Current Chapter Only</li>

      <h2>Quick FAQ</h2>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>English Proper Nouns / Names / Places Mispronounced?</summary>
        <div class="faq-answer"><p>Microsoft TTS Covers Common Proper Nouns Well. Rare Ones Fall Back to Grapheme-to-Phoneme. Fix: Note Correct Pronunciation in App Notes, or Check Forvo/YouGlish, or Submit Feedback to Microsoft TTS Team.</p></div>
      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>Dialogue-Heavy Novels (Harry Potter) — How to Distinguish Characters?</summary>
        <div class="faq-answer"><p>Single Voice Can't Voice-Act. Workaround: Export MP3 → Au/Audacity Pitch-Shift Different Characters (+3/-3 Semitones), or Accept Single Narrator + Rely on Quote Marks in Text.</p></div>
      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>Can I Export "Bilingual Alternating" Audio (CN/EN Alternating)?</summary>
        <div class="faq-answer"><p>Not Directly Supported. Workaround: Prepare Bilingual EPUB (Side-by-Side) → Export with Mixed Voices (CN Segments Xiaoxiao, EN Segments Aria) → Requires Manual Language Markers in Text, Tedious. Better: Export CN/EN Separate → Two Playlists for Comparison.</p></div>
      </details>
      <details class="faq-item">
        <summary>Can Exported MP3 Go Straight into Anki as Listening Cards?</summary>
        <div class="faq-answer"><p>Yes. Anki Template <code>[sound:Chapter_03.mp3]</code>. AwesomeTTS Add-on Batch Imports. Pro Tip: ffmpeg Split by Silence → Sentence-Level Audio → Sentence Listening Cards = Best Results.</p></div>
      </details>
