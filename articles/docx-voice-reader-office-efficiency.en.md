      <h2>Gotchas: These DOCX Won't Render Perfectly</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>❌ <strong>Encrypted/Protected DOCX</strong>: Remove Protection (Know Password) or Print to PDF → Import</li>
        <li>❌ <strong>Deeply Nested Tables / Merged Cells</strong>: Linearization May Scramble. Manual Check Critical Tables</li>
        <li>❌ <strong>Heavy ActiveX / Content Controls / Field Codes</strong>: May Read Field Codes Not Display Values. Preview Confirm First</li>
        <li>❌ <strong>Huge Docs (>200 Pages / 50MB)</strong>: Slow Parse, High RAM. Split by Chapter into Smaller Files First</li>
      </ul>
