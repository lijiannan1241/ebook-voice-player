How it works

Three steps from shelf to sound

  1. Open a book

    Pick a TXT, DOCX, PDF, EPUB or HTML file — or add a whole folder. The reader parses it locally and lays out the chapters.

  2. Choose a voice

    Browse by language and region, preview a few voices, then set the speed. Your choice is remembered for next time.

  3. Press play

    Start at the beginning or click any paragraph. Follow the highlight, bookmark a passage, or export the audio for later.

FAQ

Questions readers ask

Short, direct answers. If yours is missing, the support page has a few more.

What is Ebook Voice Player?

Ebook Voice Player is a Windows desktop application that reads e-books aloud. You open a TXT, DOCX, PDF, EPUB or HTML file, choose a neural voice, and the app reads the text while keeping the page visible and highlighting the paragraph being spoken.

Which ebook formats does it support?

It opens TXT, DOCX, PDF, EPUB and HTML files. Chapters are detected automatically where the file structure provides them, and you can also start playback from any paragraph.

Does it work offline?

Books are opened and displayed locally, but speech synthesis uses an online neural text-to-speech service, so an internet connection is required to generate new audio. MP3 files you export can be played offline in any audio player.

How many voices and languages are available?

More than 300 neural voices across 70+ languages and 140+ regional locales were available at the time of writing. The exact list depends on the online speech service and is refreshed by the app.

Can I export the narration to MP3?

Yes. Ebook Voice Player can render the selected chapters or the whole book to MP3 files, which you can copy to a phone, an MP3 player or any other device for offline listening.

What are the system requirements?

Windows 10 version 1809 (build 17763) or later, or Windows 11, on an x64 processor. The app is distributed as a signed MSIX package through the Microsoft Store and needs an internet connection for speech synthesis.

Is my book uploaded to a server?

No. Books are opened and parsed on your device. Only the text you choose to narrate is sent to the Microsoft Edge text-to-speech service to generate audio. Ebook Voice Player requires no account and includes no telemetry or advertising SDKs.
